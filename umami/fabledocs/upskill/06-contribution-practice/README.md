# 06 — Contribution Practice

Reading teaches recognition; contributing teaches judgment. This module is a graded ladder of work on Umami, from one-file fixes to multi-week projects, each with the realism a maintainer would actually expect.

Files:

1. [`01-good-first-tickets.md`](01-good-first-tickets.md) — 20 junior tickets (1 file, testable), spread across the repo.
2. [`02-mid-level-feature-tickets.md`](02-mid-level-feature-tickets.md) — 12 cross-layer tickets requiring design notes, risk, and rollback.
3. [`03-senior-build-projects.md`](03-senior-build-projects.md) — 6 projects (days–weeks) with full design/test/rollout plans.
4. [`04-refactor-and-design-katas.md`](04-refactor-and-design-katas.md) — senior refactor/design katas with self-grading.

**The contribution standard (every level):** a change must (1) be useful, (2) have a small, understood blast radius, (3) follow an existing pattern, (4) be tested, and (5) not be something a maintainer would reject for scope creep or breaking a public contract. Read [`../07-career-and-collaboration/`](../07-career-and-collaboration/README.md) before opening a real PR.

Every ticket/project carries an **Interview story potential** line — completing it gives you a concrete STAR story ([`../08-interview-prep/06-behavioral-star-stories.md`](../08-interview-prep/06-behavioral-star-stories.md)).
